# Retained Staff — HR & Payroll Module

A standing-employment booking type on SkilledProz. Hirers can add a worker
to their **payroll** (once), then pay them **repeatedly** — weekly, monthly,
quarterly, or on-demand — through the existing escrow + staged-release flow.

## Why it exists

Direct bookings and job posts are **one-off**. Retained Staff is for
**ongoing employment**: an office cleaner hired monthly, a part-time dev
paid weekly, a contractor settled at end of project. No re-booking, no
check-in, no check-out. Just hire once → pay forever.
